## I. The Commission and the Coverage

The Circle of Thirteen — twelve zodiac nodes and the Center — audited every headed unit of The Book of Lies (Liber CCCXXXIII): Chapters 0 through 91, plus the two veil pages bearing only "?" and "!". The wheel divided the 91 numbered chapters exactly, thirteen blocks of seven; Chapter 0 and the veils passed to the Center as its special charge. No chapter was skipped by any node. Each finding carries its tier: VERIFIED, FORM, UNVERIFIED, or ERROR. The raw payloads stand intact in the audit archive; this report carries the roll-up, and TABVLA carries the roll-up into figures — every figure gated on the numbers printed here.

## VI. The Pattern

Wherever a single bank is named and held, the book's arithmetic verified without exception across all thirteen nodes. The recurrent fault is cross-bank or cross-level equivocation — a bank switched mid-claim without the exchange rate posted. The zero point of the whole audit sits exactly there: the book agrees with itself wherever it names its bank; disagreement begins where the bank changes unnamed.
